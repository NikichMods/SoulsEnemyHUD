# Changelog

## 0.3.1 — 2026-10-28

First public packaged release.

- Compact enemy HP bars, accumulated damage and a separate lock-on dot.
- Bosses retain the lock-on dot while duplicate overhead HP/damage are hidden.
- Native large boss bar uses matching HP/trail colors and accumulated damage on its right.
- Decorative Aphes name above the boss bar fits within 90% of its full width and stays stable while damage changes.
- Includes the accepted soft-edged subtitle backdrop style.

Tested and visually accepted on Steam 1.09/build12996889 with UE4SS v3.0.1-1181-g83330635.
